Basement Waterproofing

Basement Waterproofing Across the GTA

Address basement moisture at the foundation with a waterproofing plan suited to your property, access, and drainage conditions.

A drier, better-protected basement

Water stains, damp walls, and recurring seepage deserve more than a temporary patch. We inspect the source of moisture and explain whether interior or exterior waterproofing is appropriate for the problem.

Solutions may include drainage paths, membrane systems, crack repairs, and foundation details designed around the structure and site.

Waterproofing may help with

  • Basement wall seepage
  • Foundation cracks and leaks
  • Wet floors after heavy rain
  • Moisture around below-grade walls
  • Drainage issues near the foundation
