Drain Repair & Construction Services

From backed up drains to wet basements, we diagnose the issue, quote clearly, and deliver code compliant work in Scarborough, East York, Ajax, Burlington, Hamilton, the Niagara Region, Milton, and Barrie.

Drain Repair

Repairs, replacements, and clearing for lines that back up or leak. From a single cracked section to a full line replacement, we diagnose with camera inspection first, then restore flow quickly and protect your foundation.

Emergency service available call for urgent flooding or sewer backups.

Waterproofing

Interior and exterior solutions to keep basements dry: membranes, drainage paths, crack repairs, and foundation details that last.

Camera Inspections

High-definition sewer and drain cameras pinpoint blockages, breaks, and offsets so you only excavate what needs attention.

Demolition

Selective interior demolition, structural removal where specified, and complete debris handling your site ready for the next phase.

Concrete & Structural Prep

Targeted concrete repairs, core openings, and prep work aligned with drainage and waterproofing one crew, one point of contact.

Estimates & Planning
